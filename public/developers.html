Authentication

            

              Send your key as x-api-key. A missing key is treated as an
              anonymous request where that is allowed; a key that is wrong or revoked is a
              401, never a quiet fallback to anonymous.
            


            
Scopes

            

              A key may only do what it was given. A call outside its scopes answers
              403 with the scope it needed in the body.
            

            

              
links:readList links and read their settings

              
links:writeCreate, update, revert and delete links

              
analytics:readStatistics, health and CSV export

              
qr:readGenerate QR codes

            


            
Endpoints

            

              
POST /api/linksCreate a link. Body: url, optional alias, title, tags, campaign, utm

              
POST /api/links/bulkUp to 250 rows. dryRun: true validates and writes nothing

              
GET /api/linksYour links, newest first. limit, cursor, q

              
PATCH /api/links/:codeChange destination, name, tags, campaign, expiry or routing rules

              
POST /api/links/:code/revertRestore a previous destination by its changedAt

              
DELETE /api/links/:codeDelete a link and its click history

              
GET /api/links/:code/statsFull breakdown for one link

              
GET /api/statsAccount totals and dimensions

              
GET /api/links/healthLinks whose destination stopped answering

              
GET /api/export?type=CSV: links, daily or campaigns

              
GET /api/qr?slug=SVG by default, format=png for a bitmap

              
GET /api/plansPlans and their limits. No key needed

            


            
Limits and errors

            

              Every key-authenticated response carries X-RateLimit-Limit,
              X-RateLimit-Remaining and X-RateLimit-Reset,
              so a client can slow down before it runs out rather than finding out at the 429.
              Errors are JSON with an error field:
              400 the request is wrong,
              401 no or bad key,
              402 your plan does not include this,
              403 the key lacks a scope,
              409 that short code is taken,
              429 too fast, with Retry-After.
