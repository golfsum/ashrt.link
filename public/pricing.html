    Questions people actually ask

    What happens when I hit the link limit?

    New links stop until the window rolls over, and the message says when that is. Every link you have already made keeps redirecting and keeps recording clicks. Nothing you have published breaks, and nothing is deleted.

    What happens if I cancel?

    Your account drops to free at the end of the period. Every link you made keeps working, including links on a custom domain you had verified, and your QR codes keep resolving. Your click history, campaigns and destinations are not deleted — the free plan shows a shorter analytics window, and the rest reappears if you subscribe again. What you lose is the ability to add new things the paid plan covered, not the things you already made with it.

    Do old links count against my monthly allowance?

    No. The allowance is on links you create in a rolling 30-day window. A link you made last year costs you nothing to keep.

    Is there a free trial?

    The free plan is the trial, and it does not expire. It is meant to be enough to see whether this works for you, on real links with real analytics.

    Does the Free plan require an account?

    Yes. Create an account and verify the email address to use the Free plan. Verification is required before permanent links and account features are enabled.
