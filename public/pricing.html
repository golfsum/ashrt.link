Free link tracker / Pricing

Every limit, before you sign up

The allowance is on links you create, not on links you keep. Anything you have already published stays live and stops counting once its month passes, on every plan, including after a downgrade.

What is in every plan

  • No interstitial. A click goes straight to the destination. No advert, no "you are being redirected" page, ever.
  • Full analytics. Clicks, unique visitors, referrers, countries, devices, browsers, operating systems, time of day and day of week. Not a paid add-on.
  • 400 days of click history, on the free plan too.
  • Bot traffic separated and named. Slack, X, Facebook, Discord and iMessage all fetch a link to build a preview. Those are counted apart from people, so your numbers are not quietly inflated.
  • A QR code for every link, pointing at the short link, so you can change the destination after it is printed.
  • Editable destinations, with the last ten remembered and one click to put an old one back.
  • CSV export of links, daily clicks and campaign totals.
  • An API with a per-day quota rather than a paywall.

What the paid plans add

A custom domain so links read as go.yourbrand.com, QR design (colours, dot and corner styles, your logo, a frame and a caption), link expiry, a much larger creation allowance, and a higher API quota. Business adds more domains and the largest allowance.

Questions people actually ask

What happens when I hit the limit?

New links stop until the window rolls over, and the message says when that is. Existing links keep redirecting and keep recording clicks. Nothing you have published breaks.

What happens if I cancel?

Your account drops to free at the end of the period. Every link you made keeps working, including links on a custom domain you had verified. You lose the ability to add new things the paid plan covered, not the things you already made with it.

Is there a free trial?

The free plan is the trial, and it does not expire. It is deliberately enough to run a real project on rather than enough to see a screenshot.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. You can create a tracking link on the home page without signing up and watch the clicks arrive. Guest links last 30 days, and you can claim them into an account later without losing the stats.
