Common questions

Do I need an account to track a link?

No. Create a link above and you get its analytics straight away through a private link only you have. A free account keeps your links permanently, puts them in one dashboard, and lets you name them and group them into campaigns.

What exactly can you see about a click?

The country, device type, browser, operating system and referring site, taken from the request itself. We do not see who the person is, and we do not store raw IP addresses. Unique visitors are counted using a one-way hash, which is enough to tell a repeat visit from a new one and not enough to identify anyone.

How is this different from a URL shortener?

The link is short, but that is a side effect. The point is the redirect: it records the visit before forwarding, which is how you find out whether the post, email or ad you shared actually sent anyone.

Will link previews inflate my click count?

No. Post a link in Slack and Slackbot fetches it to build the preview; X, Facebook, Discord, LinkedIn and iMessage all do the same. Those are identified and counted as bot hits, separately from people.

How long do links last?

Links created without an account are kept for 30 days. Save one to a free account, or create it while signed in, and it stays until you delete it.
